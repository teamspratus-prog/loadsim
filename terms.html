A picture, not a load plan

What it is

The same point the rest of the site makes, stated here so it is on the record.

A visualizer, and only that

The simulator takes the dimensions you enter and draws them. It reports how many pallet positions that fills, how much of the usable volume and payload is taken, and how much floor length, headroom and weight is left. That is the whole job.

It does not choose equipment for you, does not search for a best fit, and does not produce a load plan, a stowage plan or a securing plan. It has no opinion about which transport unit you should book. Two configurations that fill the same percentage can be completely different loads, which is exactly why the tool shows you the empty space instead of ranking anything.

Accuracy

The numbers are arithmetic, not measurements

Every figure is computed from what you typed, using the geometry of rectangles. Nothing is measured, inspected or verified. Where the tool is likely to differ from the trailer in your yard:

  • Built-in dimensions are nominal. Real interiors vary between manufacturers, between builds, and with wear, liners, reefer units and load bars. Override them with your own measurements when it matters.
  • The payload limit is a single figure. The tool applies 20,200 kg to every transport unit. Your actual limit depends on the tractor, the trailer, the axle configuration, the route and the jurisdiction, and is frequently lower.
  • Weight distribution is not modelled. The tool reports a total. It says nothing about axle loading, about where the centre of gravity sits, or about whether a legal total is legally distributed.
  • Nothing physical is modelled. Not crush strength, not overhang, not pallet quality, not stacking stability, not door clearance, not how the load behaves when the truck brakes.

The tool is provided as is, without any warranty that it is accurate, complete, error-free or fit for a particular purpose. Check every figure against the actual equipment and the rules that apply to your route before acting on it.
