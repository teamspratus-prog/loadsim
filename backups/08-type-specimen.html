The point of it

A fill percentage will not tell you where the room is

Open floor at the doors and a band of headroom over the whole load are both “23% full”, and they need opposite decisions. One takes another row; the other takes taller pallets, or nothing at all.

Scope

What it does not do

It is a visualizer. It shows one configuration at a time and draws the consequence of it. It does not choose for you.

  • It does not recommend a transport unit, a best fit or an optimal load.
  • Payload is measured against a single 20,200 kg limit for every unit.
